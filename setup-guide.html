SYNAPTIQ ARCHITECTURE GUIDE

Local-First, Privacy-First, BYO-AI Learning OS Architecture

1. LOCAL-FIRST INDEXEDDB ARCHITECTURE

All study plans, syllabus graphs, mistake records, and focus logs live locally in browser IndexedDB (SynaptiqDB). Passwords use Web Crypto API (PBKDF2 SHA-256 salted hashing).

2. BRING YOUR OWN KEY (BYOK) AI SYSTEM

Connect your own Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Anthropic keys in Settings. Keys remain encrypted on your device.
