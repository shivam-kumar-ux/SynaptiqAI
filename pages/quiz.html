NORMALIZATION DIAGNOSTIC Question 1 of 5
CONCEPTUAL QUESTION

Which normal form requires that all non-prime attributes are fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key (no partial dependencies)?

A
First Normal Form (1NF)
B
Second Normal Form (2NF)
C
Third Normal Form (3NF)
D
Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF)
WHY THIS IS CORRECT:

2NF removes partial functional dependencies where a non-prime attribute depends on only part of a composite primary key.
