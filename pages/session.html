1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Database Normalization is the process of structuring a relational database in accordance with a series of normal forms in order to reduce data redundancy and improve data integrity.

2. EXPLAINED SIMPLY

Imagine a school register where student addresses are written next to every subject grade. If a student moves houses, you would have to update 10 different rows! Normalization splits this into two tables: one for Students and one for Grades, connected by a unique Student ID.

3. EXAM DEFINITION

"A relation R is in 3NF if for every functional dependency X → Y, either X is a superkey or Y is a prime attribute."
